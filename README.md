## About

My work usually begins with a real domain problem and ends with a tested, documented product. I care about clear system boundaries, honest claims, accessible interfaces, and explaining the decisions behind an implementation.

I am currently developing my foundations in software engineering, statistics, data systems, and applied research. I am open to internships, research projects, and thoughtful open-source collaboration.

## Selected work

### [NephroCare](https://www.nephrocares.in)

A bilingual kidney-health education and preliminary screening service. It organises report values, symptom context, saved report references, and appointment questions while keeping the boundary between education and medical diagnosis explicit.

`React` `TypeScript` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Vercel`

[Repository](https://github.com/samanyuahuja/NephroCare)

### [Next Chapter Initiative](https://nextchapterinitiatives.com)

The digital platform for a youth-led nonprofit working across literacy, technology education, and access. The project includes public information, programme pages, newsletter signup, and a validated contact workflow.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Express` `Resend`

[Repository](https://github.com/samanyuahuja/NCI)

### [SamQuant](https://samquant.vercel.app)

A quantitative research system for testing strategy ideas with validated market data, delayed execution, portfolio accounting, risk controls, and reproducible analysis. Historical results are treated as research evidence rather than forecasts.

`Python` `pandas` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `pytest`

[Repository](https://github.com/samanyuahuja/SamQuant) | [Research paper](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lSlu87iYgoT5s39RK3BOaDHfhelHS8txKvTm_hgPefM/view)

## Working principles

- Separate domain logic from presentation.
- Test assumptions that can change a result.
- Prefer accurate limitations to inflated claims.
- Make the interface understandable to the person who needs it.
- Leave the repository easier to inspect than I found it.

## Current direction
